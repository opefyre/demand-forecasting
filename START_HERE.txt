DemandLab v5
============

1. Open Terminal in this folder.
2. Run: ./scripts/setup_mac.sh
3. Run: ./scripts/start_mac.sh
4. In the app: Import → Load paper & printing demo → Run forecast.

Bundled demo inputs:
- 36 months historical demand
- 6 months future assumptions
- 14 customer–SKU demand streams

All demo data is synthetic.
